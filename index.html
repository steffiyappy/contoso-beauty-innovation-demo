Exercise 1 · Morning Brief with Copilot ChatFree tier · Web-grounded · Two speeds

The scenario. It is 07:00 on a Monday. The board meeting is at 09:30. Bu Sari opens Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat at m365.cloud.microsoft/chat, verifies the green Enterprise Data Protection shield next to her name, and asks for a briefing before anyone else is in the office. She runs two variants: a five-minute quick brief for the CEO's WhatsApp, and a fifteen-minute structured brief for herself.
Task 1.1 · Quick Brief
5 minutes · Conversational · No citations
Use when you have five minutes before the CEO's WhatsApp reply
Task 1.2 · Structured Brief
15 minutes · G-C-S-E · Cited
Use when you need a full pre-board briefing table
Task 1.1💬 Copilot Chat · web-grounded · 5 min

Quick five-minute brief - conversational

Short prompts, natural conversation, no framework needed. Perfect for the WhatsApp reply.

In Copilot Chat, run these prompts in sequence:

What are the top three cosmetics industry stories in Indonesia this week? Focus on halal certification news, product launches, and any regulatory changes from BPOM. Keep it to one sentence per story.
How has the Korean won and Chinese yuan moved against the Indonesian rupiah in the last thirty days, and what does that mean for imported cosmetics ingredients? Two sentences.
Give me a two-sentence public-info background on Ibu Retno Kartini, CEO of PT Sinar Kecantikan Nusantara. Only publicly available information.
The point. Conversational prompts, no framework, no source references. Copilot Chat pulls from the public web in real time. Bu Sari copies the answers into WhatsApp for the CEO in under five minutes.
Task 1.2💬 Copilot Chat · G-C-S-E · 15 min

Structured fifteen-minute brief - full pre-board briefing

A single tabular output covering four sections. The four coloured labels below (Goal, Context, Source, Expectation) form the G-C-S-E framework - four labels that turn a vague request into a reliable output.

Goal: prepare me a Monday board-meeting morning briefing. Context: I am Executive Assistant to the Board of Directors at Contoso Beauty Innovation, an Indonesian cosmetics company with four house brands (Rosa, Amara, Kaia, Zeta). The board meets at 09:30. Two external visitors are attending: Ibu Retno Kartini (CEO, PT Sinar Kecantikan Nusantara) and Bapak Andi Prasetyo (Managing Partner, Nusantara Growth Capital). Source: use publicly available web information only. Prioritise reputable Indonesian news sources (Kompas, Detik, Bisnis Indonesia, CNBC Indonesia, Katadata) and industry outlets (Kontan, Beauty Industry Asia). Expectation: return one Markdown table with four sections, in this order: 1. Cosmetics industry news this week (Indonesia focus) - three bullets. 2. Competitor moves (Wardah, Emina, Kahf, Somethinc, YOU) in the last thirty days - one line each. 3. Regulatory pulse (BPOM, MUI Halal, LPPOM, MoT import rules) - anything filed or announced in the last two weeks. 4. Two visitor profiles - name, current role, one-line company snapshot, two recent quotes or public positions, one suggested opening question. Tone: neutral, factual, short. Add a source URL at the end of each bullet. If a fact is uncertain, mark it "[to verify]" and do not guess.
The point. Same audience, same source, different level of rigour. The four coloured labels make the framework easy to spot but stay out of the way. She spends the extra ten minutes because it will save her thirty minutes of scrambling in the meeting.

1.3 · Prompt tambahan untuk kerja harian (Bahasa Indonesia)

Enam prompt siap-pakai untuk pekerjaan sehari-hari Bu Sari, semuanya mengikuti kerangka G-C-S-E. Boleh dipakai langsung, atau diadaptasi sesuai konteks masing-masing.

Prompt 1💬 Copilot Chat · Risalah rapat direksi

Draft resolusi dewan komisaris

Goal: buatkan saya draft resolusi dewan komisaris dalam Bahasa Indonesia yang menyetujui alokasi anggaran pemasaran FY27 senilai 3,2 miliar rupiah untuk ekspansi brand Amara ke kota-kota Tier-2. Context: saya Executive Assistant to the Board di Contoso Beauty Innovation. Resolusi ini akan ditandatangani oleh empat komisaris pada rapat Senin depan. Bahasa yang dipakai harus gaya korporat Indonesia yang formal, tapi jangan berlebihan. Source: hanya gunakan informasi yang saya berikan di prompt ini. Jangan mengarang angka lain atau nama komisaris. Expectation: tiga bagian berurutan: 1. Paragraf-paragraf pertimbangan ("Menimbang bahwa...") yang merujuk ke pembahasan Q3. 2. Isi resolusi dalam klausul bernomor ("Memutuskan: Pertama, ...; Kedua, ..."). 3. Blok tanda tangan untuk empat komisaris. Nada: formal, netral, tanpa kata sifat berlebihan seperti "sangat", "signifikan", "luar biasa". Panjang maksimal satu halaman.
Prompt 2💬 Copilot Chat · Riset industri

Pemetaan pesaing untuk klaim tertentu

Goal: bantu saya memetakan langkah pesaing terkait klaim "clean beauty" dan "waterless formulation" dalam 60 hari terakhir. Context: saya menyiapkan bahan untuk rapat marketing di Contoso Beauty Innovation. Pesaing utama yang perlu dipantau: Wardah, Emina, Somethinc, YOU Beauty, Kahf. Audiens output ini adalah CMO baru yang belum familiar dengan lanskap kosmetik Indonesia. Source: gunakan sumber publik saja - media Indonesia (Kompas, Bisnis Indonesia, CNBC Indonesia, Katadata), akun media sosial resmi brand, dan situs web resmi brand. Jangan menebak. Expectation: satu tabel Markdown dengan kolom brand, tanggal, judul pengumuman singkat, ringkasan satu kalimat, URL sumber. Urutkan berdasarkan tanggal terbaru. Jika tidak ada pengumuman untuk suatu brand, tulis "Tidak ada pengumuman relevan" pada baris tersebut - jangan mengarang.
Prompt 3💬 Copilot Chat · Regulasi

Latar belakang sertifikasi halal MUI vs BPJPH

Goal: jelaskan dengan bahasa yang mudah dipahami, apa perbedaan antara sertifikasi Halal MUI dan sertifikasi Halal BPJPH di Indonesia saat ini. Context: saya perlu memberi briefing dua paragraf kepada dewan komisaris Contoso Beauty Innovation. Kami adalah produsen kosmetik, dan brand Amara saat ini masih memegang sertifikasi MUI. Anggota dewan komisaris bukan orang teknis regulasi, mereka butuh gambaran besar dan implikasi bisnis. Source: hanya gunakan sumber resmi dan publik - situs BPJPH, situs MUI, PP Nomor 39 Tahun 2021, dan pemberitaan media utama Indonesia yang relevan. Cantumkan URL sumber untuk setiap fakta. Expectation: tiga bagian, masing-masing satu paragraf pendek: 1. Perbedaan utama antara dua sertifikasi tersebut. 2. Jadwal transisi resmi dan tenggat waktu yang berlaku. 3. Implikasi konkret bagi brand kosmetik yang hanya memiliki sertifikat MUI. Jika ada informasi yang belum jelas atau masih dalam pembahasan, tandai "[perlu verifikasi]" - jangan menebak.
Prompt 4💬 Copilot Chat · Komunikasi internal

Pengumuman dwibahasa untuk internal

Goal: buatkan pengumuman internal dalam dua versi (versi Inggris dulu, lalu versi Bahasa Indonesia yang natural) untuk menyambut Chief Marketing Officer baru yang bergabung minggu depan. Context: pengumuman ini dikirim atas nama CEO Contoso Beauty Innovation ke seluruh karyawan (sekitar 12.000 orang, tersebar di R&D, pabrik, marketing, dan retail). CMO baru bernama Ibu Melissa Kartika, sebelumnya di brand kosmetik global, akan fokus pada penguatan brand Amara dan Kaia di 100 hari pertama. Source: hanya gunakan informasi yang saya berikan di prompt ini. Jangan mengarang latar belakang atau kutipan. Expectation: dua versi, masing-masing di bawah 120 kata. Versi Bahasa Indonesia harus terasa natural, bukan terjemahan kata per kata dari versi Inggris. Nada hangat tapi tidak berlebihan. Sertakan satu kalimat tentang fokus 100 hari pertamanya, dan satu kalimat ajakan untuk menyambut beliau.
Prompt 5💬 Copilot Chat · Kalender & delegasi

Menyortir tumpukan permintaan meeting

Goal: bantu saya menyortir 15 permintaan meeting yang masuk hari ini menjadi empat kategori tindakan. Context: saya EA untuk CEO Contoso Beauty Innovation. Kalender CEO Senin depan sudah 80% penuh. Saya akan menempelkan daftar 15 permintaan di bawah prompt ini - masing-masing berisi judul, nama peminta, durasi yang diminta, dan tingkat urgensi. Source: hanya gunakan daftar yang saya tempel. Jangan mengarang peminta baru atau agenda baru. Expectation: satu tabel Markdown dengan kolom nomor, judul meeting, peminta, dan kategori. Empat kategori yang harus dipakai: (a) Terima apa adanya. (b) Delegasikan - sebutkan siapa yang paling tepat (COO, CFO, CMO, atau Head of E-commerce). (c) Tolak dengan sopan - berikan satu kalimat alasan yang bisa saya pakai. (d) Butuh input CEO sebelum saya bisa jawab. Setelah tabel, berikan tiga kalimat rekomendasi tentang pola yang saya lihat dari 15 permintaan ini (misalnya: terlalu banyak dari satu fungsi, atau terlalu banyak yang bisa didelegasikan).
Prompt 6💬 Copilot Chat · Role-play persiapan

Latihan sebelum percakapan sulit dengan vendor

Goal: bantu saya berlatih sebelum menyampaikan kepada vendor lama bahwa kontraknya tidak akan diperpanjang di Q4. Context: saya EA to the Board di Contoso Beauty Innovation. Vendor ini adalah Event Organizer yang sudah kerja sama tujuh tahun, tapi kualitasnya menurun dua kuartal terakhir. Yang berhubungan dengan saya adalah Account Manager mereka, Pak Bambang, yang kenal baik dengan CEO. Percakapan akan dilakukan by phone dalam Bahasa Indonesia. Source: gunakan konteks yang saya berikan di prompt ini. Jangan mengarang detail kontrak lain. Expectation: perankan Pak Bambang. Balas seolah-olah Anda sedang menerima kabar tersebut dari saya. Beri saya dorongan balik yang realistis - misalnya menyebut hubungan jangka panjang, minta bertemu langsung dengan CEO, atau menawarkan diskon. Gunakan gaya bahasa yang wajar dipakai vendor senior Indonesia (campuran Bahasa dan sedikit Inggris, sopan tapi tidak menyerah). Lakukan tiga pertukaran dialog. Setelah pertukaran ketiga, keluar dari peran dan berikan tiga catatan singkat: (1) di mana saya kurang tegas, (2) di mana saya bisa lebih empatik tanpa berubah pikiran, (3) satu kalimat penutup yang lebih baik daripada yang saya pakai.
Kenapa kerangka G-C-S-E membantu. Empat label ini (Goal, Context, Source, Expectation) memaksa kita menyebutkan hal-hal yang biasanya kita anggap "sudah jelas" - siapa audiensnya, sumber apa yang boleh dipakai, dan seperti apa hasil yang kita inginkan. Untuk pekerjaan admin harian yang berulang, sekali kita punya prompt yang bagus dengan G-C-S-E, kita bisa pakai ulang minggu depan tinggal ganti angkanya.
Reality check. Free Copilot Chat is web-grounded only. It cannot read Bu Sari's email, files, calendar, SharePoint, or OneDrive. If she needs a brief on internal Contoso data, that requires either uploading the file into the chat (see Exercises 2 to 4) or a paid Microsoft 365 Copilot licence with in-app grounding (not in scope for this session). Enterprise Data Protection is active only when Bu Sari is signed in with her corporate Entra ID; verify the green shield icon before pasting anything that references internal names, deals, or product formulas. Public-info background on people should stay strictly to publicly available sources; do not ask Copilot for private, personal, or non-public information about individuals.

Task 2.1📝 Word Agent · Polish + restructure

Open Copilot Chat and invoke Word Agent

Open m365.cloud.microsoft/chat. Click the paperclip or drag 01 Contoso_Q3_Launch_Memo_Draft.docx into the chat. Type @ to open the agent picker, choose Word, then paste:

Goal: turn my Q3 launch memo into a board-ready two-page document. Context: audience is the Board of Directors. Company is Contoso Beauty Innovation, four house brands: Rosa (mass premium), Amara (halal masstige), Kaia (Gen-Z color), Zeta (dermocosmetic). Board members are not marketing specialists; they want clarity, not jargon. Source: the memo I just uploaded. Expectation: in this order: 1. Add an executive summary at the top: three sentences, the single most important number for each of the four brands, one sentence on the biggest risk. 2. Rewrite the body in active voice, cut every sentence to under twenty words, remove any repetition. 3. Adopt Contoso house style: short paragraphs, no adjectives like "very", "significantly", "extremely", no marketing adverbs. 4. At the bottom, add an "Action items" section: bullet list, each item has (a) what, (b) who owns it, (c) by when. If who or when is not in the memo, mark it "[to confirm]" - do not invent names or dates. 5. Do not add facts not present in the original memo.
Task 2.2📝 Word Agent · Bahasa translation

Translate the executive summary into natural Bahasa

The regional marketing team needs the exec summary in Bahasa. Same chat session, next prompt.

Terjemahkan bagian executive summary di atas ke Bahasa Indonesia yang natural dan profesional, bukan terjemahan kata per kata. Gunakan gaya bahasa yang biasa dipakai di internal memo perusahaan kosmetik Indonesia. Pertahankan angka dan nama brand persis seperti aslinya. Panjang maksimal empat kalimat.
Note. The Word Agent handles Bahasa natively. The instruction to translate "naturally, not word-for-word" is what stops it producing awkward Google-Translate output.
Task 2.3📝 Word Agent · Board Q&A prep

Anticipate the board's questions

The board will read for two minutes and then ask five hard questions. Bu Sari wants to know what they are before the meeting.

Act as three different board members reading the memo: (1) a former CFO focused on margin and cash, (2) a marketing veteran focused on brand equity and share of voice, (3) an independent director focused on ESG, halal integrity, and governance. Each will ask me the single sharpest question they would ask in the room. Give me five questions total, phrased the way a real board member would ask them in Bahasa Indonesia mixed English, plus a two-sentence draft answer for each. Do not use jargon that is not in the memo.
Reality check. The Word Agent reads the text content of the file Bu Sari uploaded. It does not save changes back to her original document; she copies the improved text into Word herself. It cannot see custom formatting, headers or footers, tracked changes, embedded macros, or images. If the memo had complex tables or comment threads, those need to be described in the prompt. File uploads to Copilot Chat are ephemeral to the conversation - if Bu Sari starts a new chat, the file is gone and she uploads again.

Exercise 4 · PowerPoint AgentRefresh the board deck · Free Copilot Chat

The scenario. Last quarter's board deck, 03 Contoso_Board_Deck_Q3.pptx, has twenty-two slides. Some are duplicates, most titles are single-word topics ("Financials", "Risks"), and there are no speaker notes. The CEO wants it repurposed for Q4 by 09:15, and might ask Bu Sari to open the meeting if he is stuck in traffic. She invokes the PowerPoint Agent.
Task 4.1 · Diagnose
Summarise, identify duplicates, propose a new opening slide
Task 4.2 · Action titles
BCG/McKinsey style, one message per slide
Task 4.3 · Speaker notes
Two-sentence notes per slide so anyone can present
Task 4.1🎨 PowerPoint Agent · Diagnose

Upload the deck and invoke PowerPoint Agent

Drag 03 Contoso_Board_Deck_Q3.pptx into Copilot Chat. Type @, choose PowerPoint. Paste:

Goal: help me refresh the Q3 board deck for the Q4 board meeting. Context: audience is the Board of Directors of Contoso Beauty Innovation. Four house brands: Rosa, Amara, Kaia, Zeta. The current deck is twenty-two slides and needs to become fifteen. Source: the deck I just uploaded. Expectation: in this order: 1. Summarise the deck in five bullets. What is the story it currently tells? 2. Identify three slides that duplicate or restate content elsewhere in the deck. Give slide number and one-line justification for cutting each. 3. Propose a new opening executive-summary slide: an action title, three headline numbers, and a one-line "so what". Use only numbers that appear elsewhere in the deck. Do not add new content that is not already in the deck. If a headline number cannot be sourced, say so.
Task 4.2🎨 PowerPoint Agent · Action titles

Rewrite every slide title as an action title

BCG and McKinsey call this the "one-message-per-slide" style. The title is the conclusion, in a full sentence. The body proves it.

For every remaining slide (after the three cuts you recommended), rewrite the title as a full-sentence action title that is the conclusion of the slide, not a topic. Rules: - The title must be a complete sentence, ideally ten to fifteen words. - The title must be sourced from data already on the slide - do not invent numbers. - The tone is neutral and evidence-based. No adjectives, no "leverage", "unlock", "synergy". Return a table with three columns: slide number, original title, proposed action title. Read only the action titles top to bottom to verify the deck tells the story without opening the bodies.
The check. After the rewrite, Bu Sari reads only the new titles top to bottom. If they tell the complete story without opening any body, the deck is consulting-grade.
Task 4.3🎨 PowerPoint Agent · Speaker notes

Speaker notes for every slide

In case Bu Sari has to open the meeting if the CEO is late.

Generate speaker notes for every slide. Rules: - Exactly two short sentences per slide. - Sentence one: what the slide is saying. - Sentence two: the transition to the next slide. - Neutral tone, first-person voice, no jargon, no "I would like to share". - If a slide has a specific number in the title, mention that number verbatim in the notes so the presenter says it out loud. Return as a table: slide number, action title, speaker notes.
Reality check. The PowerPoint Agent works on the deck's text content. It does not generate new visuals, redesign slide layouts, apply your corporate template, or fix broken charts. Any changes it suggests must be copied by Bu Sari into her actual .pptx - nothing is saved back automatically. If the deck contains complex diagrams, SmartArt, embedded video, or animation sequences, the agent may not understand them; she should describe those elements in her prompt. Fonts, colours, and master-slide styling remain her responsibility. The Word Agent, Excel Agent, and PowerPoint Agent all share the same underlying limitation: they read what is uploaded, they do not open your original files in place.

B1 · Prompt tips for the free Copilot Chat

The G-C-S-E framework

Four labels that turn a vague request into a reliable output.

  • Goal - what I want at the end (a memo, a table, a draft email).
  • Context - who I am, who the audience is, what industry, what constraints.
  • Source - which files I have uploaded, or "public web only".
  • Expectation - the structure, length, tone, and any rules (no invention, use IDR millions, cite sources).

Use it for anything with more than one deliverable. Skip it for one-shot conversational prompts.

Two universal habits

  • Say what "good" looks like. "Four sentences, one number per sentence, no adjectives" beats "make it good".
  • Say what "not to do". "Do not invent names or dates" and "do not use words like leverage, synergy, unlock" save an entire re-run.

B2 · Enterprise Data Protection - the green shield

Enterprise Data Protection (EDP) is what makes free Copilot Chat safe for internal work.

How to check

  1. Open m365.cloud.microsoft/chat and sign in with your corporate Entra ID (the @paragon-innovation.com account, not a personal one).
  2. Look at the top of the chat window. You should see a small green shield with the text "Protected" or "Enterprise data protection".
  3. If you see "Signed in with a personal account" or a grey shield, sign out and back in with the corporate account.

What EDP protects

  • Your prompts and Copilot's responses are not used to train the underlying models.
  • Data stays within Microsoft's commercial boundary.
  • Third-party plugin telemetry is restricted.

What EDP does not do

  • It does not make internal data flow to Copilot. Copilot only sees files you upload, prompts you paste, and public web results.
  • It does not stop the chat history being visible to anyone who unlocks your device. Sign out of shared devices.

B3 · Confidentiality do's and don'ts

The six rules

  1. Verify the green shield first. No shield, no internal content.
  2. Never paste unreleased product formulas, INCI ingredient lists for a launch not yet public, or supplier contract terms.
  3. Never paste employee personal data - salaries, home addresses, ID numbers, health information.
  4. Never paste customer PII in bulk - member lists, transaction logs with names.
  5. Redact before uploading. If a memo has a person's phone number and the point of the memo is the argument, delete the phone number first.
  6. When in doubt, ask. Info Security has a decision tree. Use it.

Grey areas

  • Public financials filed with OJK: fine.
  • Draft financials before the earnings release: not fine.
  • Public press quotes about a competitor: fine.
  • Private notes about a competitor from a supplier conversation: not fine.

B4 · Bahasa Indonesia prompt patterns

When to prompt in Bahasa

  • The output will be read by an Indonesian audience (memo, invitation, notulensi, WhatsApp reply).
  • The source content is in Bahasa (a memo, a news article, an interview transcript).
  • You want cultural nuance (salam, forms of address, honorifics).

When to prompt in English

  • The task is analytical (structure a table, find anomalies, summarise numbers).
  • You are mixing multi-language source material.
  • You want stable prompt patterns you can copy across contexts.

One trick that fixes Google-Translate output

When asking Copilot to translate, add: "Gunakan Bahasa Indonesia yang natural dan profesional, bukan terjemahan kata per kata. Sesuaikan dengan cara orang Indonesia berbicara di lingkungan kerja profesional. Pertahankan makna dan nada, tapi hindari frasa yang canggung atau terlalu formal."

Copilot handles this instruction consistently in both directions (ID to EN and EN to ID).

B5 · Ten one-line admin prompts to copy-paste

Everyday admin tasks. Paste, edit the specifics, send.

Draft a warm two-line thank-you email in Bahasa Indonesia to Ibu [name] for hosting yesterday's board dinner. Do not overdo the compliments. Sign off from me (Sari).
Draft a one-page meeting agenda in Bahasa for a two-hour cross-functional planning meeting on [topic]. Include: objectives, five agenda items with time boxes, expected outputs, decision points, and pre-read.
Given this three-day Jakarta-Singapore-Jakarta itinerary, produce a single-page travel summary for the CEO. Include: flights, hotel, three meeting slots per day, ground transport windows, and one line on the local currency and time zone.
Draft a formal Bahasa Indonesia invitation email for a Q4 townhall on [date] at [venue]. Include RSVP link, dress code, and a two-line agenda preview.
I have twenty-eight external emails from yesterday - here are the subject lines and senders. Categorise into (1) needs my action, (2) FYI only, (3) reply on behalf of CEO, (4) archive. Return as a table.
Write a two-sentence WhatsApp reply from me to Pak [name], gracefully declining the coffee catch-up next week because I am blocked. Suggest two alternative slots the week after.
Given this two-page policy document I just uploaded, extract every deadline, obligation, and named owner into a checklist I can share with the operations team.
Rewrite this three-paragraph LinkedIn post the CEO drafted so it reads like a thoughtful practitioner post, not a company announcement. Keep it under 200 words. Mix Bahasa and English the way an Indonesian executive would.
Suggest five gift ideas under 500,000 IDR appropriate for a female senior executive who lives in Jakarta, is over sixty, values understated elegance, and has already been given wine and flowers.
Convert this ten-minute rambling voice-memo transcript into three-bullet action items for the CEO's morning huddle.
